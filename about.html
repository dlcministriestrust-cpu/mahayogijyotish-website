About the creator

Dr. Clifford C. Smith, known as Cliff, has practiced and taught Transcendental Meditation, yoga and subtle energy healing for more than five decades, and has studied and practiced Jyotish for more than 37 years. He has devoted his life to spiritual pursuits, traveling throughout the US, Europe, India and Asia to conduct healing tours and to study with spiritual teachers, yoga masters and master healers.

He trained personally with Maharishi Mahesh Yogi and became a teacher of Transcendental Meditation. His other teachers include Dr. Willard Fuller of Florida, an interfaith Christian minister known worldwide for his spiritual dental healings and the founder of the Lively Stones Fellowship; Swami Kaleshwar of Penukonda, India, known for his powerful mantra healing teachings; and Swami Vishwanath Saraswati, also a powerful mantra healer, regarded as the fifth incarnation of Lord Dattatreya. It was Swami Vishwanath who gave him the spiritual name Mahayogi.

Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish, the system he developed, pairs a special numerology-based casting formula with a largely traditional reading of Vedic astrology. It grew out of deep meditative study and his recognition of a mathematical correlation between the structures of Nature and the Vedic literature.

He offers the Calculator to give help and guidance to all who seek it.
