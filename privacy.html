Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

The Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish Calculator (the "Calculator") is provided by the DIVINE LIGHT CHRISTIAN MINISTRIES TRUST (the "Owner," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account, send sign-in links and service notices, and send occasional news and offers from the Owner. Every news email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Your purchase history and how many questions you have left, so we can provide what you paid for and process refunds.
  • The link that brought you to us, such as a referral partner's link, so we can credit our referral partners.

Information we do not collect

  • Your questions and charts. We do not save them. Monthly subscribers may save their charts to their own device; those copies stay with you.
  • Your card details. Payments are handled by Stripe and Link, which process and store card information under their own privacy policies. Card details never reach us.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Calculator, and only as needed for them to do so:

  • Render, which hosts the Calculator;
  • Resend, which sends our sign-in and service emails;
  • Stripe and Link, which process payments, sales tax, and refunds. Link is the seller of record and sends your receipt;
  • Cloudflare, which counts visits to our website.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information where required by law, or to a new owner if the Calculator is sold, as described in the Terms of Use.

AI interpretations

Your chart is interpreted by an AI assistant you choose. Whatever you send to that service is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

Videos

Videos on this website and in the Calculator play through YouTube. If you play one, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies, visit counting, and Do Not Track

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors; it only tells us how many people visit and which pages they view. Because we do not track you across websites, we do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your information, except purchase records we must keep for tax and legal purposes.

Children's privacy

The Calculator is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the Calculator from outside the United States, you consent to this transfer.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from news emails at any time, ask us to correct your email address, or ask us to delete your account and the information above by contacting us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or through the Calculator. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
