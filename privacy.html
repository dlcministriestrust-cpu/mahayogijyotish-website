Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

The Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish Calculator (the "Calculator") is provided by the DIVINE LIGHT CHRISTIAN MINISTRIES TRUST (the "Owner," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Our promise: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. If the business is ever sold or transferred, your information may pass to the new owner only under this same Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and send sign-in links and service notices, and, only if you choose to opt in, occasional special offers and news. See Communications below.
  • Your purchase history and how many questions you have left, so we can provide what you paid for and process refunds.
  • The link that brought you to us, such as a referral partner's link, so we can credit our referral partners.

Information we do not collect

  • Your questions and charts. We do not save them. Monthly subscribers may save their charts to their own device; those copies stay with you.
  • Your card details. Payments are handled by Stripe and Link, which process and store card information under their own privacy policies. Card details never reach us.

Communications

We send transactional communications relating to your account and purchases, such as sign-in links, gift notices, and service notices. If you choose to opt in, we may also send you promotional communications, including special offers relating to the Calculator and news about other products and services we develop. You may opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in each such message, by changing your choice in your account, or by contacting us at support@mahayogijyotish.com. Opting out will not affect transactional communications, which are necessary to provide the Calculator and your account.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share personal information only: (a) with service providers that perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing, hosting, email delivery, and website analytics, and only to the extent necessary for them to provide those services; (b) when required to do so by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request; and (c) in connection with a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or other business transfer, in which case your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, and any successor will be bound by this Privacy Policy. We may share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

Our service providers are:

  • Render, which hosts the Calculator;
  • Resend, which sends our sign-in and service emails;
  • Brevo, which keeps our mailing list and sends offer emails to those who opt in;
  • Stripe and Link, which process payments, sales tax, and refunds. Link is the seller of record and sends your receipt;
  • Cloudflare, which counts visits to our website.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy, security or data practices of these third parties.

AI interpretations

Your chart is interpreted by an AI assistant you choose. Whatever you send to that service is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

Videos

Videos on this website and in the Calculator play through YouTube. If you play one, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Cookies, visit counting, and Do Not Track

We use only essential cookies, or similar storage in your browser, that keep you signed in, remember the link that brought you to us, and protect the Calculator against misuse. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe and Link may set their own cookies on their checkout pages, under their own privacy policies. This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors; it only tells us how many people visit and which pages they view. Because we do not track you across websites, we do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your information, except purchase records we must keep for tax and legal purposes. After deletion we keep only a one-way code made from your email address, which cannot be turned back into it, so the free first question can't be claimed twice.

Children's privacy

The Calculator is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the Calculator from outside the United States, you consent to this transfer.

Your choices

You may opt out of offer emails at any time, ask us to correct your email address, or ask us to delete your account and the information above by contacting us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or through the Calculator. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
