Frequently asked questions

Do I need my birth time or birth chart?

No. Prashna reads the moment of the question, and Ankha Jyotish draws that moment from the number you choose.

How do I choose my number?

Hold your question lightly in mind and choose whatever number from 1 to 108 comes to you. Don't calculate it or pick a favorite.

Will the same number always give the same chart?

No. Your number starts the chart, but the Calculator draws the rest fresh each time you ask, so ask each question sincerely.

Who writes the interpretation?

An AI does. The Calculator casts the chart and prepares instructions for reading it, including this system's own rules when the chart needs them. With one tap you copy both, ready to paste into Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity or another AI you trust. The AI then writes your reading by the classical rules of Vedic astrology. A free account with any of these is enough.

What kinds of questions can I ask?

Life questions: work, money matters, relationships, family, travel, a move, a decision. Ask one clearly formed question at a time, about a real situation that exists now rather than a hypothetical one.

Can it tell me the future for certain?

No one can promise certainty, and predicting outcomes is the most challenging part of Vedic astrology. Even so, this system is designed to show likely events and outcomes, and you may be amazed at how well it does. But even the best Jyotishis are sometimes wrong, and so is AI. The more background you give the AI about your question, the more personal and accurate its answer tends to be. Use the reading as guidance alongside your own judgment, not in place of it. It does not replace the advice of a doctor, lawyer, financial adviser or any other professional.

What does the free question include?

One full chart, once per email address, with no time limit.

How long do paid questions last?

Single and five-pack questions have no time limit while the Calculator is in service. The monthly plan gives up to 20 questions each month; unused ones expire at the end of the month. Monthly plans renew each month until you cancel, which you can do yourself at any time.

What if I run out of monthly questions early?

While your monthly plan is active, you can buy extra questions at half price: one for $2.50 or five for $6. Each add-on lasts 30 days from purchase and is not refundable. Your plan still renews on its usual date.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of purchase. No refunds are given after 14 days. Any questions you have already used are counted at the single price of $5 each, and you get the rest back. For example, if you bought the $12 five-pack and used 2 questions: 2 × $5 = $10, so $12 − $10 = a $2 refund. The same rule applies to the monthly plan: 2 questions used that month means $19 − $10 = a $9 refund. Subscriber add-ons are not refundable. Full details are on the Pricing page.

Can I give questions as a gift?

Yes. From your account in the Calculator you can send one question or a five-pack to someone else. We email it to you, and you forward it or text it to them. When they open it, a gift box unwraps, and the questions are theirs, with no time limit. You can cancel a gift for a full refund within 14 days of buying, but only until your friend collects it. See Gifts for the details.

Do you keep my questions?

No. We don't save your questions or charts. Monthly subscribers can save their charts to their own device for future reference.

Is this numerology or astrology?

Both. Your number opens the chart, and the chart is read as Vedic astrology. It uses the Vedic (sidereal) zodiac and the classical rules of Parashari Jyotish, plus a few additional rules for arrangements unique to Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish.

Are these the planets' real positions in the sky?

No. The Calculator shows each planet's energetic influence in a sign and house, not where it stands in the sky today. That is why a Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish chart can show placements a sky-based chart cannot, such as one planet present in more than one house at once.

Why does my chart show only a few planets?

In Prashna, the answer to a question rests mainly on a few key planets: the ones that stand for you, for the matter you're asking about, and the Moon. The Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish Calculator, built on the foundation of classical Prashna, shows just these, so your reading stays clear and focused. Nothing is missing.

Can I ask the same question again?

Yes. If the situation hasn't changed, the chart should give essentially the same answer, though often through a different arrangement of planets, and the AI may emphasize different points the second time. Situations can change quickly, so asking again later can show how things have moved. For the clearest answer, wait a while before asking again. Avoid asking just to test the Calculator or trip it up. A question asked to test it carries a different intent than a sincere one, and it may not give a good answer. An ambiguous question may give an ambiguous answer. Frame your question so it is clear what you are asking, and don't combine several questions into one. If an answer is unclear, ask the AI you use to clarify it. If it is still unclear, rephrase your question and ask again.
