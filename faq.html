Frequently asked questions

Do I need my birth time or birth chart?

No. Prashna reads the moment of the question, and Ankha Jyotish draws that moment from the number you choose.

How do I choose my number?

Hold your question lightly in mind and choose whatever number from 1 to 108 comes to you. Don't calculate it or pick a favorite.

Will the same number always give the same chart?

No. Your number begins the chart, but the Calculator draws the rest fresh each time. Ask each question sincerely.

Who writes the interpretation?

An AI does. The Calculator casts the chart and writes instructions for reading it, adding the system's special rules whenever the chart calls for them. With one tap you take both to Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity or another AI you trust, which writes the reading by the classical rules of Vedic astrology. A free account with any of these is enough.

What kinds of questions can I ask?

Life questions: work, money matters, relationships, family, travel, a move, a decision. Ask one clear question at a time, about a real situation that exists now rather than a hypothetical one.

Can it tell me the future for certain?

Certainty in anything is hard to come by, and predicting future outcomes is the most challenging part of Vedic astrology. Even so, this system is designed to show high-probability events and the likely outcomes of situations, and you may be amazed at how well it performs. Still, even the best human Jyotishis sometimes get it wrong, and so can AI. The more background you give the AI about your question, the more personal, focused and accurate its answer tends to be. Use the system as a tool for guidance alongside your own judgment, not in place of it. It does not replace the advice of a doctor, lawyer, financial adviser or any other professional.

What does the free question include?

One full chart, once per email address, with no time limit.

How long do paid questions last?

30 days from purchase. Monthly plans renew each month until you cancel, and you can cancel yourself at any time.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, for unused questions within 14 days of purchase, less a $1 processing fee. A monthly plan is refundable only if no question was used that month.

Do you keep my questions?

No. Your questions and readings are not saved by us. Monthly members can download their readings to their own device.

Is this numerology or astrology?

Both. Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish is the union of numerology and Jyotish: your number opens the chart, and the chart is read as Vedic astrology. It uses the Vedic (sidereal) zodiac and mostly the classical rules of Parashari Jyotish, with a slightly modified rule set for arrangements unique to Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish.

Are these the planets' real positions in the sky?

No. The Calculator shows each planet's energetic influence or presence in a sign and house, not where it stands in the sky today. That is why a Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish chart can show placements and combinations a sky-based chart cannot, such as one planet present in more than one house.

Can I ask the same question again?

Yes. If nothing has changed with the situation, the chart should basically give you the same answer, often through a different arrangement of planets, and the AI may choose to emphasize different elements the second time. Sometimes situations change quickly, so asking again later can show how things have moved. For the clearest answer, ask sincerely and wait a while before asking again. Asking again right away usually comes from testing or dissatisfaction, trying to force the answer, and that is not the same question with the same intent. If an answer is unclear, refine the question instead: you may have lumped several questions together, or asked something vague or ambiguous. Rephrase and ask again.
