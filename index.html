A question asked sincerely already carries its answer.

That is the ground of Prashna, the branch of Vedic astrology that reads a chart for the moment a question is asked rather than for the moment of birth. You need no birth time and no birth chart, only a real question.

Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish is a number-based form of Prashna, the union of numerology and Jyotish. Instead of reading the clock, the chart is drawn from a number the questioner chooses. The number is the questioner's own contact with the moment, and the chart that grows from it is read mostly by the classical rules a Jyotishi would use for any Prashna, with a slightly modified rule set for arrangements unique to Mahayogi Ankha Jyotish.

The Calculator does the heavy work for you

  • Type your question and choose your number, called the Alpha number, from 1 to 108
  • The Calculator casts the chart: signs, houses, the planets that stand for you and for the matter asked, their strength and condition, and the deeper divisional chart that shows how things ripen
  • One tap copies the finished chart, with instructions for reading it, to Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or any AI you prefer, which writes the reading in plain words. The instructions include the system's special rules whenever the chart calls for them

Use it for the questions life actually brings: work, relationships, a move, a decision you cannot settle. The Calculator gives guidance, not certainty, and is not a substitute for medical, legal or financial advice. Always apply common sense to any answer given.
