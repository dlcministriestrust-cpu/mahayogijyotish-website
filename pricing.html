Single and five-pack questions have no time limit while the Calculator is in service. Monthly questions reset each month: unused ones expire at the end of the month. Sales tax, where it applies, is added at checkout.

Refunds

You can ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase, pro-rated at $5 per question used (the single price). No refunds are given after 14 days. Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Here is exactly how it works:

  • Single question ($5): if you have not used it, you get your full $5 back. Once used, it cannot be refunded.
  • Five-pack ($12): every question you have already used is counted at the single price of $5 each. That amount is taken off what you paid, and you get the rest back.
  • Monthly plan ($19): if you have not used any question that month, you get your full $19 back. Once a question has been used that month, the month cannot be refunded.

Example: five-pack, 2 questions used

You paid: $12
2 used questions × $5 = $10
$12 − $10 = $2 refund

Five-pack refunds at a glance: 0 used = $12 · 1 used = $7 · 2 used = $2 · 3 or more used = no refund

To request a refund, use the Refund my unused questions button in your account at app.mahayogijyotish.com. It works out the amount for you using these rules.

Gifts

You can give questions as a gift from your account in the Calculator: one question ($5), a five-pack ($12) or a month of questions ($19, up to 20 questions). Gifts are sold at the regular prices, and sales tax is added at checkout.

  • Delivery: at checkout you choose how it's sent. We can email it to your friend as soon as your payment goes through, or email it to you so you can forward it or text it yourself, whenever you like. Either way, you get an email with the gift. If we send it for you, let your friend know to look for it, and to check their spam folder if it's not in their inbox. If we send it, they sign in with the email address you gave us. If you send it yourself, their email stays private, and whoever opens the gift and signs in collects the questions, so send it only to the person you intend it for.
  • No time limit: gift questions have no time limit while the Calculator is in service. A gifted month is one month of questions, starting from your friend's first question. It does not renew, and you are not charged again.
  • Refunds go to you, the buyer: within 14 days of buying, pro-rated at $5 for each question your friend has used. A gifted month can be refunded only if your friend has not used any of it. Use the same Refund my unused questions button in your account. Once a gift is refunded, its unused questions are removed from your friend's account.

Your privacy

We don't save your questions or charts. Monthly subscribers can save their charts to their own device for future reference. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
