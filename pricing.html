Run out before your month is up? While your monthly plan is active, you can buy extra questions at half price: one question for $2.50 or five for $6. Each add-on lasts 30 days from purchase and is not refundable. Your plan's renewal date doesn't change.

Refunds

Cancel within 14 days of purchase and you get back what you paid, minus $5 for each question used (the single price). No refunds are given after 14 days. Refunds go back to the card you paid with. Here is exactly how it works:

  • Single question ($5): if you have not used it, you get your full $5 back. Once used, it cannot be refunded.
  • Five-pack ($12): every question you have already used is counted at the single price of $5 each. That amount is taken off what you paid, and you get the rest back.
  • Monthly plan ($19): every question you have already used that month is counted at $5 each. That amount is taken off the $19, and you get the rest back. A refund also ends your monthly plan.
  • Subscriber add-ons ($2.50 / $6): not refundable, since they are sold at half price.

Example: five-pack, 2 questions used

You paid: $12
2 used questions × $5 = $10
$12 − $10 = $2 refund

Five-pack refunds at a glance: 0 used = $12 · 1 used = $7 · 2 used = $2 · 3 or more used = no refund

To request a refund, click Refund this next to the purchase in your account at app.mahayogijyotish.com. It works out the amount for you using these rules.

Gifts

You can give questions as a gift from your account in the Calculator: one question ($5) or a five-pack ($12). Gifts are sold at the regular prices, and sales tax is added at checkout.

  • Delivery: we email the gift to you, and you forward it or text it to your friend whenever you like. Whoever opens the gift and signs in collects the questions, so send it only to the person you intend it for.
  • No time limit: gift questions have no time limit while the Calculator is in service.
  • Once it's collected, it's theirs: when your friend opens the gift and signs in, the questions belong to their account, under their email address, and the gift is complete. It can't be refunded after that.
  • Refunds before it's collected: until someone collects the gift, you can cancel it within 14 days of buying for a full refund, back to your card. Sign in with the email address you bought it with, open your account, and click Refund this next to the gift.

Your privacy
